AI product

A working front-end prototype that demonstrates product thinking: a focused user problem, a small MVP, explainable output and a clear path toward a production system.

Interactive Prototype · AI Product

RoleFit AI

Problem: Job seekers often know they are interested in a role but cannot quickly see how their evidence maps to the requirements.

Solution: RoleFit AI compares a candidate profile with a job description and turns the gap into prioritized skills and actions.

Product thinking: Start with a narrow workflow, make the output explainable, and give the user an immediate next action instead of a generic “AI score.”
